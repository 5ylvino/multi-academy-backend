# MAS Support FAQ

## Parent portal and fees
- Parents open the Parent portal to view fees, payment history, and receipts.
- Online fee payment uses the school's configured payment provider (Paystack or Flutterwave).
- If payment fails, confirm the invoice is still open and retry from Fees in the portal.

## Attendance alerts
- Schools can enable absence alerts so parents are notified when a child is marked absent.
- Attendance is entered by staff; parents can view history in the portal.

## Student results and report cards
- Results appear after teachers enter scores and approvers publish report cards.
- Parents see only their linked children.

## Account activation
- Parents may need a school-generated activation code to link to their child.
- Contact the school administrator if activation fails.

## AI assistant scope
- The AI assistant answers school operations questions only: fees, calendar, attendance, and how-to guidance.
- It cannot change grades, fees, or records through chat.
